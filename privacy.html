1. Introduction

ConForge AI LLC (“ConForge,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) operates WorkForge, an AI-powered personal assistant designed to help individuals and businesses manage everyday work, coordinate information, and complete tasks across their existing applications.

WorkForge includes ForgeClaw, an assistant for everyday professional tasks, and ForgeMeet, an assistant focused on meetings, summaries, and follow-up actions. WorkForge may be accessed through supported desktop applications, mobile applications, web interfaces, and integrations such as Microsoft Teams.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, access, process, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, use WorkForge, connect third-party applications, or communicate with us.

We recognize that an assistant used for professional work may encounter confidential business information, personal communications, documents, calendars, and meeting content. Our approach is to provide useful functionality while respecting the permissions, systems, and privacy choices of our users.

WorkForge is designed to work with your existing applications and systems rather than requiring you to move all your business information into a separate ConForge-controlled content repository.

By using WorkForge, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular processing activity, we will obtain that consent separately.

The information involved in your use of WorkForge depends on the features you use, the applications you connect, the permissions you grant, and the configuration selected by you or your organization.

We may collect information needed to create and administer an account, including:

Depending on the integration and features enabled, this may include:

  • Email messages, subject lines, senders, recipients, and relevant metadata.
  • Attachments and communication history relevant to a task.
  • Drafts, replies, and messages available through supported integrations.

Calendars and scheduling

  • Calendar events, dates, times, and availability.
  • Meeting invitations, descriptions, and attendee information.
  • Scheduling preferences and relevant event details.

Documents and files

  • Documents, attachments, file contents, and file names.
  • File locations, metadata, and access permissions.
  • Information retrieved to answer questions or complete tasks.

Meetings

  • Meeting titles, schedules, and participant information.
  • Transcripts, notes, or recordings where supported and authorized.
  • Meeting summaries, decisions, and follow-up actions.

Other business applications

  • Records, tasks, and information from supported business systems.
  • Information required to complete a user-requested operation.

WorkForge does not necessarily access all these categories for every user. Access depends on the particular integration, the permissions granted, and the features enabled.

5. How We Use Information

We use relevant information for the following purposes.

5.1 Providing WorkForge

We process information to:

  • Create and administer accounts.
  • Connect authorized applications.
  • Interpret user instructions.
  • Retrieve information needed to answer questions.
  • Generate drafts, summaries, recommendations, and other outputs.
  • Support email, calendar, document, and meeting-related tasks.
  • Prepare meeting summaries and follow-up actions through ForgeMeet.
  • Complete supported tasks through ForgeClaw and other enabled features.
  • Provide personalization and continuity where supported.

5.2 Performing User-Requested Actions

Depending on the feature and permissions, WorkForge may prepare or carry out tasks through connected applications.

These tasks may include preparing communications, organizing information, scheduling events, or updating records.

WorkForge is designed so that actions which send, accept, book, or otherwise commit on your behalf are prepared for your review and completed only after you approve them. If you or your organization choose to set up standing permissions for specific, routine tasks, WorkForge will act only within the scope of those permissions. The available controls depend on the feature and the connected application.

You should review permissions and task instructions carefully, particularly when an action could affect another person, change a business record, or create an external commitment.

5.3 Security and Reliability

We may process technical information to authenticate users, protect accounts, detect suspicious activity, diagnose errors, maintain availability, prevent abuse, and enforce our agreements.

5.4 Customer Support

We use support-related information to answer questions, troubleshoot problems, investigate reported issues, and communicate important service updates.

5.5 Legal and Compliance Purposes

We may process information when reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, respond to valid legal requests, establish or defend legal claims, or protect the rights and safety of ConForge, our users, and others.

We do not assume that information accessible through a connected application may be used for unrelated purposes simply because the connection makes it available.

5.6 No Use of Your Content to Train AI Models

We do not use your business content, prompts, files, meeting information, or the outputs generated for you to train artificial intelligence models, whether ours or anyone else's. Where WorkForge relies on third-party AI model providers to deliver its features, we work with those providers under terms and settings intended to ensure that your content is not used to train their models.

5.7 AI Services

WorkForge includes an AI assistant. When you use it, we send the following information to our AI service provider, OpenRouter, Inc., which passes it to an AI model provider such as Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google to generate a reply:

  • The messages you type to the assistant.
  • The work information the assistant looks up to answer you, such as meeting transcripts and notes.

We use this information only to answer your requests and write meeting notes. OpenRouter and the model providers process it under zero data retention: they do not store it after generating a response and do not use it to train AI models. They protect your data at least as well as we do.

In the WorkForge phone app, no information is sent to these services until you allow it on the Assistant screen. You can withdraw permission at any time in Account > Privacy & Data > AI Assistant.

Dictation and transcription on your phone run on the device and are not sent to an AI service.

6. Data Storage and Processing

6.1 Our Approach to Storage

WorkForge is designed to operate with your existing applications and systems. ConForge does not intentionally maintain a separate content repository for the business information you connect to or submit through WorkForge.

This means WorkForge is designed to access and process relevant information to perform requested tasks without requiring you to move all your business content into a separate ConForge-controlled storage system. Your business content remains in the systems that you and your organization control, such as your connected email, calendar, document, and meeting applications. ConForge retains only the limited operational information described in Section 6.4, which is needed to run and protect the Services.

This statement concerns the storage of user content. It does not mean that no information is ever processed, transmitted, or temporarily held while a service operates.

6.2 Temporary Processing

To provide WorkForge functionality, information may need to be retrieved, transmitted, or temporarily processed.

For example, a request may require retrieving a relevant email, processing meeting information, or returning a generated response through the WorkForge interface.

Temporary processing is distinct from maintaining a permanent content repository. The systems involved may handle limited information as technically necessary to complete the requested operation.

6.3 Information in Your Existing Systems

Information in connected email accounts, calendars, document platforms, meeting services, or other applications remains subject to the storage and retention practices of those systems.

WorkForge may create or update information in those systems when you authorize an action. Such information may remain there according to your settings and the policies of the relevant service.

ConForge does not control the independent retention or deletion practices of those third-party services.

6.4 Operational Information

We may process limited account, authentication, technical, diagnostic, and security information as reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, and protect WorkForge.

This operational information is distinct from the business content you access through connected applications.

The exact information handled depends on the configuration and technical requirements of the Services.

9. Meeting Information and ForgeMeet

ForgeMeet supports meeting-related work, including meeting notes, summaries, decisions, and follow-up actions.

Depending on the feature and configuration, ForgeMeet may process meeting titles, schedules, participant information, transcripts, recordings, notes, and other meeting-related material.

We do not assume that every meeting feature records or transcribes every meeting. Processing depends on the feature enabled and the information made available to it.

ForgeMeet records only when you choose to start a recording. Where supported, speech is converted to text on your device, and recordings are stored in encrypted form so that they are accessible to the user who made them, subject to your device settings and any controls established by your organization.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights, permissions, and consents to record, transcribe, upload, or otherwise process meeting information, including information about other participants.

You must comply with applicable recording laws, workplace policies, contractual confidentiality obligations, and organizational requirements.

Generated summaries and action items may omit context or misinterpret statements. Review important outputs before relying on or distributing them.
